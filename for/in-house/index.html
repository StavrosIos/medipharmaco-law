Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity from the first conversation

Here are some of the things we often get asked when speaking to life sciences companies. If your question is not listed, please get in touch.

What does Medipharmaco Law do?+

Medipharmaco Law provides specialist legal and compliance support to organisations operating in the pharmaceutical, biotech and wider life sciences sector. Our consultants help companies navigate complex regulatory environments, manage legal risk, and maintain effective legal and compliance functions.

Who do you typically work with?+

We support a wide range of life sciences organisations, from large pharmaceutical multinationals to growing biotech companies that may not yet have an in-house legal function. Our clients operate globally across sectors such as pharmaceuticals, medical devices, R&D and consumer health.

How is Medipharmaco Law different from a traditional law firm?+

Unlike traditional law firms, Medipharmaco Law consultants have predominantly worked in-house within life sciences organisations. This means we understand the commercial realities of the business and provide practical, business-focused solutions rather than purely theoretical legal advice.

What type of legal support can you provide?+

Our consultants support organisations across a wide range of areas including regulatory compliance, clinical trials, healthcare professional interactions, intellectual property collaborations, contract management, supply chain legal issues, and data privacy.

Can Medipharmaco Law support our in-house legal team?+

Yes. Many of our clients already have an in-house legal or compliance team. We provide interim support, project assistance, or specialist expertise to help teams manage workload spikes, cover team attrition, or deliver strategic initiatives.

Do you offer outsourced legal services?+

Yes. For organisations that do not have their own legal or compliance department, Medipharmaco Law can operate as an outsourced legal and compliance function, providing experienced professionals on a flexible basis.

How flexible is your support?+

Our services are designed to be flexible and scalable. We offer ad-hoc support, project-based work, interim secondees, and subscription models so organisations can access legal expertise when they need it.

Where are your consultants located?+

Medipharmaco Law works with a global network of legal and compliance experts across regions including the UK, EU, US, Middle East, Australia and Asia-Pacific, allowing us to support organisations operating internationally.

Why was Medipharmaco Law founded?+

Medipharmaco Law was created from more than twenty years of GC-level experience across private practice and in-house healthcare and life sciences roles, alongside a medical student’s perspective on medicine, science and clinical AI. The shared purpose was to build a practice grounded in interdisciplinary expertise and the realities of the sector.

How do we get started with Medipharmaco Law?+

You can book a call with one of our experts to discuss your current challenges. We will assess your needs and recommend the most appropriate level and type of legal or compliance support.
