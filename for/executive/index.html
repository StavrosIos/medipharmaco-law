Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity from the first conversation

Here are some of the things we often get asked when speaking to life sciences companies. If your question is not listed, please get in touch.

Ask us a question
Can you help if we do not have an in-house legal team?

Yes. We provide flexible legal and compliance support for organisations without a dedicated internal function, with engagement models shaped around the capacity and expertise required.

Can you support an existing team?

Yes. We can add specialist knowledge or capacity when internal teams face complex initiatives, increased workload or gaps in expertise.

What kind of business decisions can you support?

Our Industry Counsel covers regulatory strategy, contracting, product development, clinical operations, commercialisation, market entry and risk assessment.

Can you help us strengthen compliance and data governance?

We assess gaps, develop policies and controls, design governance frameworks and train operational teams handling sensitive, regulated or clinical data.

Can we access medical or scientific expertise?

Our Medical & Scientific Expertise Desk brings clinicians, scientists and technical experts into concept review, validation, evidence support and product strategy.

How do we begin?

Speak to a specialist about your priorities. We will identify the relevant expertise, define the scope and recommend an appropriate engagement model.
