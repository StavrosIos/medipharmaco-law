Frequently Asked Questions

Clarity from the first conversation

Here are some of the things we often get asked when speaking to life sciences companies. If your question is not listed, please get in touch.

Can you help with a medical claim?+

Our Medico-Legal support includes negligence and malpractice matters, liability mitigation, defence strategy and expert instructions.

Can you help us instruct a medical expert?+

Yes. Our medico-legal capabilities include expert instructions, supported by access to clinical and scientific subject-matter expertise.

Can you support an investigation after a clinical incident?+

We support internal investigations, enforcement matters, clinical risk management and governance. Training and templates also cover incident response and investigation readiness.

Do you advise healthcare organisations on compliance?+

Yes. We support hospitals, clinics and care networks with regulatory strategy, operational compliance, patient safety, clinical services contracting and governance.

Can you help with patient data and clinical AI?+

Our Data Governance and Clinical AI Expertise Desks support sensitive clinical data, AI validation, governance, risk assessment and responsible clinical deployment.

How do we get started?+

Speak to a specialist about the matter and the support required. Please do not send confidential information through the initial enquiry form.
