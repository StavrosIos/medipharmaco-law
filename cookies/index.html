Cookies Policy

Last updated: September 2026

1. Scope

This policy explains how this website uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies that store information on or access information from your device. These technologies are governed by UK data-protection and electronic-communications rules.

2. Current Use

The website does not currently load analytics, advertising, or cross-site tracking services. Optional analytics and marketing categories are off by default. If our use changes materially, we will update this policy and request a fresh choice before activating newly introduced non-essential technologies.

3. Essential Preference Storage

When you make a privacy choice, the website stores a first-party local-storage record named mpl_consent_v1. It records the consent-policy version, your analytics and marketing choices, and a timestamp. It is used only to remember the choice for up to six months and is treated as essential preference storage.

4. Optional Analytics

Analytics technologies would be used only to understand aggregate website use and improve performance. No analytics provider is currently installed, and analytics remains disabled unless you actively select it.

5. Optional Marketing

Marketing technologies would be used only for advertising or cross-site measurement. No advertising or marketing technology is currently installed, and marketing remains disabled unless you actively select it.

6. Your Choices

On a first visit, you can accept optional categories, reject them with equal ease, or manage categories separately. Rejecting optional technologies does not prevent access to the website. You can reopen the privacy centre at any time by selecting Cookie preferences in the footer. A later choice replaces the earlier one.

7. Browser Controls

You can also delete website data or block storage through browser settings. Clearing local storage will remove the saved preference and cause the privacy banner to appear again. Browser controls may affect features on this or other websites.

8. Contact and Updates

We periodically review the technologies used by this website. The updated date on this page identifies the current version. Questions may be sent to contact@medipharmacolaw.com.
