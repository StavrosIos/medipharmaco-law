Overview

Flexible, high-calibre legal capacity for in‑house teams during periods of increased workload, limited resources, hiring freezes, interim coverage, maternity leave, or gaps in expertise. A plug‑and‑play solution that integrates seamlessly into your existing legal function — exactly when, where, and for as long as you need it.

Detailed capabilities

  • Interim legal counsel for short‑ or long‑term coverage
  • Overflow support during peak workload periods
  • Specialist expertise for niche or complex matters
  • Contract drafting, review, negotiation, and management
  • Policy development and internal governance support
  • Cross‑functional collaboration with compliance, HR, procurement, and product teams
  • Rapid onboarding to minimise disruption and maximise continuity

Why Medipharmaco Law

  • Deep experience supporting in‑house teams in healthcare and life sciences
  • Ability to integrate seamlessly into existing workflows and culture
  • Immediate deployment with minimal ramp‑up time
  • Senior‑level counsel without the cost or delay of permanent hiring
  • Flexible engagement models tailored to your operational realities
  • Proven track record of stabilising teams during high‑pressure periods
