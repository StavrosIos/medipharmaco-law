Overview

With practical, in-house style expertise across governance, risk and operations, we devise specialist, streamlined data privacy processes and policies, designed to help life science organisations ensure personal data is handled compliantly, and with confidence.

We design, refine, and implement data governance frameworks, policies, and operational processes. Our team trains your staff and supports ongoing governance to ensure responsible, secure, and compliant use of data — including sensitive, regulated, and clinical data.

Detailed capabilities

  • Data governance framework design and implementation
  • Data lifecycle mapping and classification
  • Drafting policies, standards, and data handling procedures
  • Clinical and regulated data governance support
  • Data stewardship models and role definition
  • Training for data owners, stewards, and operational teams
  • Risk assessments, controls, and monitoring mechanisms

Why Medipharmaco Law

  • Unique blend of legal, regulatory, and technical data expertise
  • Deep understanding of clinical and sensitive data environments
  • Ability to translate complex requirements into practical workflows
  • Proven methodologies for building sustainable governance structures
  • Strong alignment with global data protection and AI governance trends
  • Trusted by organisations handling high‑risk, high‑value data assets
